READING THE DRAWER

Keep the collecting bias in view.

  • Places people visit get recorded more often than difficult terrain.
  • Institutions and projects choose particular taxa, dates and methods. Citizen-science projects can dominate modern records.
  • Digitization is uneven. A specimen can be old while its online record is new.
  • This is a capped API-order sample. Its proportions do not represent the full GBIF query.

Coverage and date meanings

Historic records are preserved specimens from 1700–1999; modern records include all record types from 2000 onward. The current collector retains up to 300 records per time band, at most 900 per region/era. The order comes from GBIF and is not random. The count reported by GBIF can change during collection.

Recorded date refers to the field event, not digitization. Captured is when this app fetched the records. Built is when the static site was assembled. Older retained captures can be selected above; source totals in the capture remain those seen at collection time.

Coordinates, licences and privacy

Only public coordinates are queried. Precise coordinates and verbatim localities are discarded. Public coordinates are rounded to a 0.02° grid; records with source withholding or generalization fields are not mapped. Rounding adds uncertainty and does not recover hidden locations.

Data licences vary by source. Each card links to its GBIF record and dataset; CSV includes attribution and licence information, but omits coordinates. CC BY-NC data has noncommercial restrictions. Consult the original source before reuse.

Filtering runs in your browser and is not sent to GBIF. The page loads its own static files and CoolCode’s aggregate visit counter. Following external record links contacts those sites. No local preferences or search terms are saved.
